In our 2024 simulation the AI beat the weekday/weekend rule by about 1–2% of electric-chiller energy in cooler months and by ~0% in hot months. In hot months tomorrow's need approaches the tank size, so the forecast has little room to save. On a single day either strategy can come out ahead: cold left in the tank at 21:00 counts as spent, so one day's % can differ a lot from the season average, in either direction (SIMULATED).
